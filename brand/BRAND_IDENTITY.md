# XENIUR brand identity

## Core idea

XENIUR turns intent into a spatial, inspectable object. The identity should feel constructed,
precise and alive without looking like a conventional automation dashboard.

## Mark

`XR_logo_vector.svg` is the primary mark. Its intersecting strokes express two things becoming
one: human intent and digital capability. Keep the original proportions and lime fill. Do not
outline it, add glow, place it in a badge, or redraw it as an app-generic monogram.

Clear space around the mark should be at least one quarter of its displayed width. At small sizes,
use the mark alone. At larger sizes, pair it with the XENIUR name in a compact constructed sans.

## Color

The application is light: warm paper, white cards, graphite ink.

- Intent lime: `#C6F939` — a fill only, always carrying dark ink (`#14180A`)
- Accent ink: `#3F6212` — the brand as text, icons and focus rings (lime is too pale to read on paper)
- Graphite ink: `#171B18`
- Page: `#F3F2EC`
- Card surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Inset surface: `#F7F6F1`
- Raised surface: `#ECEBE3`
- Secondary ink: `#474E48`
- Structural line: `#DCDAD0`; control border: `#85827A`

The logo mark sits on a small graphite tile so its lime stays true on a light page.
Every ink holds WCAG AA (4.5:1) on every surface it can sit on; control borders hold 3:1.

The state signal is lime's deep ink (`#3F6B0B`). It identifies the selected Living Object, a
current trustworthy state, or work actively in flight. Rebuild blue and review amber remain
semantic status colors and never borrow the lime.

## Typography

Use a constructed, condensed sans for names and decisive labels. The application stack is
`Avenir Next Condensed`, then `Arial Narrow`, then `Roboto Condensed`. Use `Avenir Next` or a
neutral system sans for prose and SF Mono or Menlo for identifiers and measurements.

Headlines are compact, heavy and tightly tracked. Prose stays sentence case. Technical labels can
use small uppercase text when they carry real state, but not as decoration.

## Shape and material

The logo's clipped diagonals inform focused-object frames. Main cards use 4px corners, controls use
3px corners, and status tags may be fully rounded. Surfaces are mostly flat graphite separated by
hairlines. Depth comes from hierarchy and overlap, not large shadows or neon glow.

## Motion

Motion only explains hierarchy, feedback or changed state. Layout never rearranges itself while a
person works. Review pulses use opacity only and stop under reduced-motion preferences.

## Voice

Use direct language that names the thing and the consequence. Prefer "Review this decision" to
"Explore insights". Never claim authorship, ownership or originality from similarity. Process
provenance reports recorded facts and leaves legal judgment to people.
