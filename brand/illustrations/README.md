The first two were regenerated as requested: only men, completely blank faces without eyes, noses, mouths, eyebrows, or beards. The third uses the same direction. The earlier detailed scenes served as composition references; the final workshop established the style reference for the other two.

## Art direction

Refined Mediterranean editorial illustration with flat human silhouettes, dry print texture, sculptural cream architecture, olive foliage, and a bright lime route. Palette anchors: ivory `#eef0e9`, cream `#e6e1cc`, graphite `#273229` / `#3b4b3e`, sage `#93a184`, lime `#c6f939`, warm ochre skin shapes, and restrained pale blue water. Natural proportions and posture communicate the activity. No text is baked into the images.

The direction combines contextual product illustration from [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations/illustrations) with consistency and simplified visual-aid principles from [GOV.UK](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/images/). This is a creative direction, not an empirical claim that a style is universally attractive.

## Additional scene briefs

The three additional original PNGs were generated with the built-in image generation tool. They use the existing illustration palette and shared lime-path motif. No labels or logo are embedded; UI controls remain HTML/SVG.

- **Source garden:** a close worktable with papers, an open botanical book, mineral samples and a magnifying lens. Masculine hands arrange sources, with no faces present. Cream limestone, olive foliage and distant coastal water connect it to the original XENIUR world.
- **Open horizon:** three cream limestone arches open toward coastal water, a reading room and a workshop. An atlas and compass sit in the foreground. One man is viewed from behind with no facial features. A lime route connects exploration back to making.
- **Made with care:** masculine hands tie a sage linen band around a finished cream document. Paper, restrained paint swatches and a small wooden structure sit on the workshop table. A lime route leads toward the open workshop and coastal horizon. No faces, words or pseudo-writing.

All scenes use matte paper texture, natural light, restrained sage and graphite, warm cream stone, quiet pale blue and lime accents. Their role is contextual orientation at an entry or stopping point. They never sit behind an editor or stand in for scientific evidence.
