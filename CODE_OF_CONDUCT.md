# Working together

Help people finish useful work. Be respectful, give specific feedback, and make room for different experience levels.

Critique an idea or implementation without attacking the person. Harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, and sharing someone else's private information are not welcome.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, or limit participation. Explain moderation decisions when doing so would not expose private information.

For conduct concerns, ask a maintainer for a private reporting channel. Do not post a person's private information in an issue. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
